07 / BUILD PLAN

Two-week production

Build the argument. Then test whether players feel it.

Week 2 creates the complete short experience. Week 3 freezes scope, tests fairness and interpretation, and refines only what the evidence says is unclear.

WEEK 2BUILD
  1. Foundation · Days 1–2

    Fork the working three-depth prototype. Preserve movement, jump, collision, plane changes, responsive input, and central audio manager. Remove foundry-specific content.

  2. Reality · Days 2–3

    Build one believable route, shallow fog, the first door, an uncertain figure, and no more than two anomalies.

  3. Hallucination · Days 3–4

    Add authored phantom platforms, false connections, advance tells, a correction route, and the transition fall.

  4. Lost Reality · Days 5–6

    Recombine existing systems into solid clouds, looping architecture, repeated doors, and a readable impossible route.

  5. Full arc · Day 7

    Connect the three stages, ending, checkpoints, and state-based sound transformations. Lock feature scope.

WEEK 3TEST
  1. Readability

    Can players identify the active plane, safe surfaces, usable doors, and the subtle tell on false platforms?

  2. Fairness

    Does failure feel authored and informative—or cheap, inconsistent, or visually misleading?

  3. Concept

    Before explaining the pitch, ask players what changed and what they trusted. Record their language.

  4. Sound

    Do repeated and displaced sounds create uncertainty without obscuring jumps, damage, or menu feedback?

  5. Technical

    Test keyboard, controller if available, phone landscape, GitHub Pages, itch.io embed, reloads, and every transition.

Definition of done

Four deliverables protect the scope.

Playable arc

Opening, three short states, recurring door, and ambiguous ending work in one uninterrupted run.

Fair illusion

At least one phantom platform surprises once, communicates why, and feels learnable on replay.

Sensory shift

Art and sound clearly progress from source clarity to uncertainty to transformed causality.

Portable build

Static relative-path package runs locally, on GitHub Pages, and inside an itch.io HTML5 upload.

Test script

Ask for observations before explanations.

READABILITY

What looked usable?

Have the player point to safe, uncertain, and impossible-looking surfaces after each stage.

FAIRNESS

Why did you fall?

If the answer is “the game broke,” the visual tell or consequence needs revision.

CONCEPT

What changed?

Listen for perception, doubt, changing rules, competing realities, or trust without supplying those words.

EMOTION

What kept you moving?

Curiosity should dominate Reality, uncertainty Hallucination, and disoriented awe Lost Reality.

STOP

Scope boundary

Week 3 is not another feature sprint.

No additional world, combat system, inventory, dialogue tree, or procedural hallucination enters the build after the full arc is playable. Refinement is the work: better tells, cleaner pacing, stronger sound, fewer bugs, and a fairer read.
