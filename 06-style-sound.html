06 / STYLE + SOUND

Sensory direction

Recognizable → ambiguous → unbound.

Image and sound follow the same arc. Reality has a clear source. Hallucination makes the source uncertain. Lost Reality disconnects effect from ordinary cause—without weakening game feedback.

Visual language

Atmosphere with authored readability.

#070913 · INK#1A2440 · NIGHT#82E9EE · SIGNAL#AD8CFF · DRIFT#E790BD · DOUBT
REALITY

Restrained contrast

Believable stone, grounded horizons, shallow fog, and one anomaly per composition.

HALLUCINATION

Conflicting evidence

False affordances, displaced objects, ambiguous silhouettes, and color that begins crossing layers.

LOST REALITY

Impossible clarity

Spatial contradiction becomes bold and legible: solid clouds, looping structures, repeated doors.

Interface + motion

The frame can drift. Feedback cannot.

INTERFACE

Minimal and stable

Navigation, input hints, focus states, and status feedback use high contrast and fixed positions. The interface never “hallucinates” or withholds essential information.

Reliable UI preserves the game’s fairness contract.
MOTION

Slow, causal transformation

Fog drifts; forms align; platforms erode with a visible cause. Avoid constant shake, random glitch, and decorative motion that competes with timing.

Reduced-motion settings remove nonessential parallax and reveals.

Sound progression

Use the same recordings three different ways.

Reality

Sound has a visible source and a dry position.

single footstep · grounded wind · direct door creak · close landing

Hallucination

The source repeats, arrives late, or moves without explanation.

echoed footstep · slight pitch drift · delayed door · reversed tail

Lost Reality

Familiar audio detaches from the object that once caused it.

long spatial reverb · reverse fragments · impossible distance · layered motifs

Small production vocabulary

A dozen clean sounds can build the whole arc.

MOVEMENT

Body

Footstep, jump, land, fall.

WORLD

Space

Wind/fog bed, distant impact, low environmental hum.

MOTIFS

Meaning

Door open/close, figure cue, depth shift.

FEEDBACK

Rules

Collect, damage, menu move/select—kept crisp in every state.
